    Your best lineups right now

    Leveling order for your heroes

      How hero investment actually works

      • Pick three to five heroes and stay loyal. Spreading shards and gear across ten heroes is the most common reason accounts feel weak.
      • Rally leader gets everything. The host's three heroes use all their skills and stats. Joiners only add the first skill of their first (lead) hero, so a joiner's lineup is one hero deep.
      • Widgets (exclusive gear) only fire when you lead a rally or garrison a rallyable building. Solo hits and joining don't use them. Only buy widgets for heroes you will lead with.
      • Hero levels raise troop capacity, so keep your three main heroes level-capped before pushing stars on side heroes.
      • New generation every few months. Your newest-gen archer is usually your biggest damage upgrade. Older-gen heroes still work as joiners and garrison fillers.
      Town Center

      The road from TC 23 to Truegold

      TC 23 is upper mid game. The true end game starts at TC 30, when Truegold levels (TG1 and up) open. Everything here is about getting there with no idle builders.

      1. TC 23 → 25: unlock Governor Charms
        Charms add Health and Lethality to every troop and are a permanent power source. TC 25 is the first big milestone above you.
      2. Upgrade only what the next TC level asks for
        Tap the Town Center's upgrade button and read the requirements. Build those buildings first. Skip side buildings until TC 30 unless they're cheap or required.
      3. TC 25 → 28: Academy and Embassy stay level with TC
        The Academy level caps your research. The Embassy speeds every timer through alliance help. Both are also required for the first Truegold level.
      4. TC 28 → 30: the expensive stretch
        These levels cost a lot of resources and time. Gather constantly, keep both builders busy, and time the TC 29 and TC 30 upgrades for a construction scoring day so the same speedups also earn event rewards.
      5. TC 30 → TG1 and up (Age of Truegold, about server day 70+)
        Needs Embassy 30 and Academy 30 first. Truegold costs climb each level (roughly 132, 158, 238, 280, 335 Truegold for TC TG1 to TG5, per community calculators).
      6. TG5 and the War Academy (about server day 220+)
        War Academy unlocks T11 troops through Truegold Dust research. It is very costly. Many F2P players push T10 troops to TG8 instead, which community testing says beats T11 at TG5 in most fights.

      Building faster without spending

      • Ask for alliance help on every upgrade before you touch a speedup. A max-level Embassy means more helps per timer.
      • Start long builds before you sleep, short ones while you're active.
      • Never let a builder sit idle. If the TC is waiting on resources, use the second builder on a requirement building.
      • Rent the second builder (about 1,000 gems for 2 days) only during a push window like Strongest Governor construction day or KvK Day 1.
      • Construction speed research (Tooling Up) and the construction speed buff pay for themselves on every upgrade.
      Academy

      Research that pays back

      The academy is a long game. Research that speeds up other research pays off on every project after it, so it comes first.

      Priority order

        Picking within the Battle tree

        • All-troop nodes first: Assault Techniques (lethality) and Survival Techniques (health) help every march.
        • Lethality before Attack, Health before Defense. They multiply better at your stage.
        • Archer stats next if you lead Bear rallies. Infantry stats next if you garrison or fight Vikings.
        • Economy tree: only gathering speed and load. Resource output research gives very little at your level.

        Be more productive in the academy

        1. The academy is never idle
          An empty research queue is the biggest loss. Before logging off, queue the longest research you can afford.
        2. Help first, speedups second
          Request alliance help right after starting. Every help cuts the timer.
        3. Time big finishes for scoring days
          Strongest Governor and KvK prep each have a research day. Save research speedups for it and finish your longest projects then.
        4. Cheap power, fast
          Many low-level nodes give 10,000+ power for one or two hours of research. Clear them during power events.
        5. Keep the Academy level matched to TC
          Academy level unlocks higher research tiers and is required for TC 30 → TG1.
        6. Use the research speed buff during long projects
          Activate it when a multi-day project starts, not on a short one.
        Gear, Charms, Widgets, Pets

        Best gear and upgrade order

        Four separate systems. Each one has its own materials, so you can push them in parallel.

        Governor Gear TC 22

        Attack and Defense. Materials: Satin Threads, Gilded Threads, Artisan's Vision.

        • Order: Infantry → Archer → Cavalry.
        • Keep pieces close in level so set bonuses stay active.
        • Upgrade during Strongest Governor / Hall of Governors gear days for double value.
        • Joiners: modest investment. Rally leaders: push hard.

        Governor Charms TC 25

        Health and Lethality. Materials from the Crystal Cave in Mystic Trial and events.

        • Order: Infantry → Archer → Cavalry.
        • Bring every charm to level 3, then push key ones to 5.
        • If you run your own Bear rallies, move Archer Lethality up.

        Hero Gear

        Enhancement, Mastery Forging (Forgehammers), Imbuement at TC 30.

        • Gear goes on your top three heroes only.
        • Push one piece to a milestone, then move to the next, instead of spreading levels thin.
        • Forgehammers are the scarcest material. Save them for Mastery Forging on Mythic gear, ideally on a KvK prep day that scores them.

        Widgets (Exclusive Gear)

        275 widgets take one hero to level 10.

        • Only active when you lead a rally or garrison a rallyable building.
        • Joiners: skip widgets until your lead heroes are done.
        • Widget stats multiply, so a few levels on your main leader is worth more than spreading them.

        Pets

        Unlock by server age. Each new pet needs the previous one levelled.

        • Lion is the first real priority (around server day 113): its passive drops feed gear, Truegold and charms.
        • Lion needs Moose at level 15. Mighty Bison needs Giant Rhino at 30. Plan one pet ahead.
        • Save Advanced Taming Marks for KvK prep pet day (about 15,000 points each).

        Troops

        • Keep all three training buildings running nonstop.
        • Promote old tiers instead of training fresh troops when you need power fast.
        • Truegold troop levels and T11 (War Academy) come after TC 30.
        • Keep hospital capacity above the troops you risk in a fight.

        Power

        Grow power efficiently

        Power is a score, not strength. The goal is the cheapest real stats per resource. Here is where your resources go furthest at TC 23, best first.

        1. Clear cheap research nodes
          Low-tier nodes you skipped give large power for minutes of research. Fastest power per speedup in the game.
        2. Town Center and its requirement buildings
          Each TC level is a large power jump and unlocks the next tier of everything else.
        3. Level your three main heroes to cap
          More troop capacity per march and more stats. Hero XP is cheap compared to shards.
        4. Promote and train troops at your highest tier
          Promotion converts old troops for less than fresh training. Do it on troop scoring days.
        5. Governor Gear and Charms
          Big stat gains, slower power. Good long-term value.
        6. Hero stars and widgets
          Biggest real combat value for rally leaders, but shard-limited.
        7. Pets
          Steady, cheap, and Lion pays back materials.
        Double-dip rule: every one of these also scores in Strongest Governor, KvK prep or Alliance Mobilization. Line up the action with the event day and you get the power and the event rewards from the same resources.
        Avoid power traps: resource-output research, upgrading every decorative building, and training low-tier troops you'll never fight with all raise power but not strength.

        Gems and packs

        Where gems and money go furthest

        Best value

        1. Permanent second builder (around $2.99). The single best purchase in the game.
        2. Hero Roulette for a hero you will actually lead with. F2P guides suggest about 70% of gems here.
        3. VIP time (about 20% of gems) for the queue and stat buffs.
        4. Temporary builder rental (about 10%) only during push windows.
        5. For low spenders: one monthly card plus the event pass you can actually finish.

        Skip these

        • VIP XP purchases
        • Basic hero pulls
        • Shop refreshes
        • Buying speedups or resources with gems
        • Chasing a leaderboard rank you can't reach. It burns the stockpile you need for the next event.

        Your data

        Can this guide read my game?

        No, and nothing safely can. Here's why, and the quick way to give me exact numbers instead.

        Why your phone's game files won't help

        • Your account lives on Century Games' servers, not on your phone. The app's local files are mostly cached art and settings, not your heroes, research or gear.
        • Reaching app files on Android needs root access, and on iPhone it isn't possible without jailbreaking. Both put your phone and account at risk.
        • Third-party tools that read or change game data break the terms of service and can get your account banned. Don't install anything that asks for your login.

        What works instead

        1. Fill in the profile on My Plan
          TC level, server age, hero generation, role and spending. That tunes everything in this app.
        2. Send Claude screenshots
          Profile page, your hero list with stars and levels, Academy research trees, Governor Gear, Charms and your bag (speedups, Truegold, shards). Claude can read the numbers and give you a plan for your exact account.
        3. Or copy your snapshot below
          Paste it into a chat with Claude along with any extra details.
